Neon Bay is an original top-down 2D open-world crime game that plays offline in a desktop browser from static files. A sun-soaked coastal city to explore on foot and by car, with traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal and drive, a wanted level with police cruisers and foot cops who chase and arrest, three story missions with cash rewards, taxi fares, fists and pistol combat, health and armor, a HUD with a minimap and a clock, start/pause/game-over menus, a localStorage save, and synthesized WebAudio sound and music. Every name, district, character and story beat is original.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 and press ENTER to play. WASD or arrows walk and drive, E enters and exits a car, J shoots the pistol, K punches, Q saves, P pauses, M mutes.

Three story missions pay $150, $300 and $500: First Fare in the yellow taxi, the Palms Cash Run, and the Heat Run. Taxi fares, cash pickups, armor, health and ammo are scattered around the city, and the wanted level (0-5 stars) rises on crime and fades when the police lose sight of you.
